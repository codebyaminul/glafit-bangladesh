Battery & Power ⏱ 10 Mins · Easy

01. Bike Won’t Power On

Diagnose a glafit that remains completely inactive when pressing the handlebar power button.

5 Resolution Steps · LFP System
Typical Symptoms
Display remains completely dark Zero throttle or motor response No LED indicator activation
Tools You'll Need
Clean dry lint-free cloth Original glafit 230V charger Multimeter (optional)
Safety First: Turn the bike key off before removing the battery pack. Never short the battery connector pins. Stop immediately if you observe swelling or heat.
Step-by-Step Resolution
1
Confirm the battery charge
Connect the original 230V charger and verify the charger LED turns solid red. Allow at least 15 minutes of charging if the battery has entered sleep mode due to deep discharge.
2
Reseat the battery pack
Unlock the battery compartment latch, pull the battery out smoothly, inspect the bottom copper terminals for road debris, and firmly slide it back into position until it clicks audibly into the locking latch.
3
Release safety interlocks
The motor controller prevents boot-up if the kickstand is extended. Fully raise the side stand, squeeze and vigorously release both brake levers to free any stuck sensor.
4
Inspect main harness quick-connect
Trace the heavy-gauge waterproof power lead below the head tube. Ensure the threaded coupling ring is screwed tight and the arrow alignment markings match.
5
Power on correctly
Turn the mechanical key to ON position, then press and hold the power button on the handlebar display cluster firmly for 2 full seconds until the glafit logo boots.
Verification Checklist
Digital LED cluster lights up clearly Battery percentage reads accurately Throttle initiates smooth motor rotation
Brakes & Sensors ⏱ 15 Mins · Easy

02. Brake Light Stays On

Release a stuck mechanical lever or magnetic cut-off sensor that inhibits electric motor acceleration.

4 Resolution Steps · Safety System
Typical Symptoms
Rear 12V red brake lamp stays constantly illuminated Motor cuts out or refuses to provide drive assist Cluster shows brake symbol
Tools You'll Need
Clean microfiber cloth Degreaser or contact cleaner spray 4mm hex key
Safety First: Never ride in traffic if the motor cut-off remains stuck. Ensure the bike cannot roll unexpectedly before testing.
Step-by-Step Resolution
1
Check both brake levers
Examine both left (rear drum) and right (front hydraulic disc) levers. Gently push each lever forward away from the handlebar grip. If the brake light turns off, the mechanical return spring has accumulated friction.
2
Clean the pivot joint
Dhaka road grime and fine sand frequently accumulate in the pivot bushing. Spray contact cleaner around the lever pivot pin and wipe away accumulated grit with a cloth.
3
Check sensor alignment
Inspect the small cylindrical reed switch housed below the lever perch. Ensure the sensor magnet has not shifted. The gap should not exceed 1.5mm when the lever is fully released.
4
Restart and test motor response
Cycle power key to OFF and back to ON. With the bike on center stand, gently twist throttle. Motor assist should activate immediately when brakes are untouched.
Verification Checklist
Tail lamp illuminates ONLY when lever is squeezed Motor cuts off instantly when brake is pulled Brake warning icon disappears from cluster
Display & Cluster ⏱ 20 Mins · Moderate

03. Display Blank or Flickering

Diagnose digital LED cluster screen issues, waterproof harness pins, and moisture infiltration.

4 Resolution Steps · Electrical System
Typical Symptoms
Screen goes blank during rides LED segments flicker over potholes Speedometer reads 0 km/h while moving
Tools You'll Need
Dry microfiber towel Dielectric silicone grease Flashlight
Safety First: Never use sharp metal picks inside multi-pin quick-disconnect connectors. Turn off the master key before unplugging.
Step-by-Step Resolution
1
Rule out low battery voltage
If the 51.2V LFP battery drops below 44V, the internal DC-DC converter may drop the 12V cluster voltage. Verify battery charge at a charging station before opening wiring.
2
Dry display housing after heavy rain
Monsoon water droplets can collect along the lower bezel. Gently dry around the rubber seal with a microfiber towel. Do not aim high-pressure water jets at the cluster.
3
Inspect 5-pin waterproof connector
Locate the green/black circular waterproof plug approximately 10cm below the handlebar clamp. Unscrew the locking collar, separate the plug, verify pins are straight, and firmly reconnect aligning the interior guide groove.
4
Perform handlebar rotation test
Turn the handlebars slowly from lock-to-lock (fully left, then fully right). If the screen flickers during rotation, check if the wiring loom is pinched near the steering head lock.
Verification Checklist
Screen stays steadily lit through full handlebar swing Speedometer responds accurately on test roll Waterproof connector collar is securely hand-tightened
Wheels & Tires ⏱ 30 Mins · Moderate

04. Fix a Roadside Flat Tire

Safe roadside puncture resolution for 14" Tubeless 90/90 R14 commercial tires on Dhaka roadways.

4 Resolution Steps · Chassis System
Typical Symptoms
Loss of tire pressure or soft sidewall Heavy sluggish steering feel Audible hissing near tire tread
Tools You'll Need
Tubeless tire repair kit & plug strip T-handle spiral reamer & insertion tool Portable 12V or hand pump with gauge
Safety First: Immediately pull off high-speed roadways. Place the bike on its dual heavy-duty center stand on a flat, level surface away from bus lanes.
Step-by-Step Resolution
1
Move bike to a safe recovery spot
Shift to a shaded, stable sidewalk or service station. Deploy the center stand so the affected 14" tire is off the ground and can rotate freely.
2
Locate foreign puncture object
Rotate the tire slowly. Inspect the thick rubber tread for embedded nails, wire, or shards. Use pliers to extract the object while noting the entry angle.
3
Insert vulcanized tire plug
Insert the spiral reamer into the puncture hole to clear steel belt fibers. Thread a rubber vulcanizing strip into the eyelet needle, apply rubber cement, push firmly into the tire until 1/3 remains, and pull tool straight out. Trim excess rubber flush.
4
Inflate and verify bead seal
Connect pump to the Schrader valve. Inflate to 32 PSI (front) or 35 PSI (rear with cargo). Apply soapy water to verify zero air bubbles emerge from the plug.
Verification Checklist
Tire pressure holds steady at 32-35 PSI No bubbling or air leakage around repair strip Smooth rolling with zero rim vibration
Lighting & 12V ⏱ 10 Mins · Easy

05. Headlight Does Not Turn On

Restore 12V LED projector and integrated daytime running light (DRL) illumination.

3 Resolution Steps · Electrical System
Typical Symptoms
LED projector headlight dark in night riding DRL ring does not illuminate Indicators or horn may still function
Tools You'll Need
Clean cloth Flashlight
Safety First: Avoid looking directly into the projector aperture when testing high beams. Disconnect power key before reaching behind front cowl.
Step-by-Step Resolution
1
Verify switch state and ignition
Confirm the ignition key is switched ON. Check the left handlebar rocker switch. Ensure it is firmly engaged into the forward position (position I for DRL, position II for high-intensity projector).
2
Check 2-pin waterproof headlight lead
Reach directly behind the metal headlight mounting bracket. Trace the black shielded wire to the round waterproof connector. Push both halves firmly together and tighten the threaded lock ring.
3
Restart auxiliary DC-DC circuit
Turn the master key to OFF, wait 5 seconds for the internal DC-DC converter capacitors to reset, then switch back to ON. Test the high/low beam selector switch.
Verification Checklist
Bright, focused white LED beam illuminates ahead High-beam blue indicator shows on digital display
Mechanical Care ⏱ 20 Mins · Easy

06. Clean & Lubricate the Chain

Quiet a dry drivetrain, prevent roller friction, and extend sprocket lifespan in urban dust.

4 Resolution Steps · Drivetrain Care
Typical Symptoms
Squeaking or metallic chirping while riding Visible red surface rust on chain links Stiff sluggish pedal resistance
Tools You'll Need
Stiff nylon chain cleaning brush Lint-free cotton shop rags Dedicated synthetic e-bike chain lubricant
Safety First: Keep fingers strictly away from rotating chainrings and sprockets. Always rotate pedals backward by hand—never engage motor power while servicing the chain.
Step-by-Step Resolution
1
Dry-brush the chain thoroughly
Deploy center stand. Rotate the pedals backward while holding the nylon brush against the chain rollers to dislodge dry sand, road mud, and dirt particles.
2
Apply lubricant link-by-link
Shake the lubricant bottle. Place the applicator tip directly over the inner roller row. Slowly rotate the pedals backward, applying one drop to each individual roller pin.
3
Allow lubricant penetration
Leave the bike stationary for 5 to 10 minutes so the synthetic lubricant wicks deep into the inner bushings and O-rings.
4
Thoroughly wipe excess surface oil
Wrap a clean dry rag loosely around the chain and backpedal 10 revolutions. Removing excess oil is vital; wet surface oil attracts abrasive Dhaka road dust.
Verification Checklist
Completely silent, smooth chain travel No excess oil flinging onto rear wheel or brake drum
